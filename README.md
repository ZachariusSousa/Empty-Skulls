# Empty Skulls

A fast-paced permadeath dungeon crawler set in a post-apocalyptic world where magic meets technology.
You play as a robot vessel, remotely controlled by the player, sent to cleanse a corrupted land. But this machine isn’t mindless it watches, learns, and adapts from your every action. When you step away, it continues your mission, fighting, exploring, and evolving on its own.

Developed solo in Unity 6 with C#, featuring:

Procedural dungeon generation and dynamic enemy spawners

Leveling up through experience and obtained upgrades

Rarity-based loot, consumables, and equipment

Real-time combat with physics-driven projectiles and screen-space effects

Experimental AI companion trained with PyTorch, capable of learning and mimicking player behavior (TBI)

(In development — blending fast-paced roguelike gameplay with emerging AI learning systems.)
